# The calibration bank guards against harm and does not prove a gain

A change to the engine's score is judged on the calibration bank (positions from real games, each scored by the engine) and the holdouts (games the weights were never fitted on: Doubles OU, VGC, the two Champions families). Game outcomes depend on dice and on player errors, so an effect of a few basis points (bp, hundredths of a percent of Brier score) does not resolve: the bank resolved a pooled effect of about 23 bp when round 48 measured it, and T52, T224 and T163 measured and adopted nothing. The decision (verdict rule of 19.09.2026, restated by the user on 22.10.2026): a candidate passes when the bank shows no harm and the tier census holds, and a gain counts only where a pooled row (the bank's average over all positions) sits wholly on the good side of its 90 % bootstrap band, or on the fit corpus out of sample in every seed. Harm means a pooled row at least `HARM_MIN_BP` (5 bp) worse with its band wholly on the bad side; the same finding in a phase cell is a warning. A rule that is right by the game stays even when the bank reads it worse, because then the weights are wrong and a refit becomes a TODO (user gate 06.10.2026: STAB by the game's rules and published Smogon sets stay; the gate of 08.10.2026 repeated it for STAB). A refit or a new K (the steepness of the curve from score to win probability) runs only where the bank reads harm after a game-correct change, never to look for a gain. The game's rules and the expert pins decide a feature; the bank and the holdouts check it.

## Considered options

- The bank as the judge of every feature (the practice before round 63): the round 63 gate offered to park an objectively correct rule (STAB with Tera) for its bank cost, and the user rejected that on 06.10.2026: a wrong measurement or wrong weights get corrected, never the rule. The same logic kept the Choice Scarf inference of round 37 (+25 bp in the late phase of the hq table against a registered line of +10 bp, verdict taken 05.09.2026) because the sets were verifiably truer.
- Sign accuracy as the main measure: it cannot see calibration, because it is invariant under monotone mappings (calibration-honesty design, 08.08.2026). The early gate there read "no bucket's sign accuracy drops by more than 2 points"; the 5 bp pooled rule is the later form.
- A verdict on point estimates: rows with few moved positions swing by single basis points, so round 49 added the size threshold.
- Bank and holdouts that build teams their own way: round 52 aligned the bank with the app's own team builder although a paired run showed harm, and booked +11 bp as the new baseline.

## Consequences

Some game-correct changes land with a bank cost and a refit TODO (T309 after T307; round 86 closed T309 without a refit, because neither the VGC fit side nor a fresh holdout repeated the harm). A pooled row of +0 bp can hide single rows, so score movements are counted per row (round 77 lesson 2). Round 54 parked the STAB rule for its bank cost (branch `r54-stab`, gate 21.09.2026); round 63 reversed that and adopted it, and the refit became a TODO. A holdout that judges round after round can drift: in round 86 the VGC holdout, in service since round 65, read the wave with the hashed tree seeds (T323) as harm (hq +18, luck-adjusted +21 bp), while 1,245 fresh games of the same seven formats, drawn by `scripts/fresh-holdout.mjs` and read against a rule fixed before the run, read them better (full -15 bp, no harm). `holdout-vgc2` then replaced `holdout-vgc` as the VGC judge; the old sets stay in the manifest, and fits keep dropping them.
