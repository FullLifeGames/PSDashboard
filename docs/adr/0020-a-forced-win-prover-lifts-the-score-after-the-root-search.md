# A forced-win prover plays the position out after the root search and lifts the score by the proven mass

Where the board is small (up to three living bodies, or the last pair) or the decided sweep or the near stage names a side and the search already reads it at `PROVER_SCORE_FLOOR` or beyond, the app plays each answer through the simulator to the end of the game after the root search and says "X wins in N against every reply", with the dice share in the sentence ("if the 95 % Fire Fang lands", "barring a crit"). The proof is an AND/OR search over the children that the endgame solver builds (`endgame/`), with a memo on the endgame key and a budget counted in matrix cells (`PROVER_BUDGET`: 100 cells per position, probe included; a greedy probe of at most 16 cells and 5 turns per side decides whether a full proof starts; 200 states, depth 12, no wall clock, so results stay deterministic). The proven mass (the share of dice outcomes along which the side wins whatever the opponent answers) lifts the score: `score = mass * sign + (1 - mass) * openValue`, where the open branches keep the static value. Matrix, ranking and interval stay as the search left them. A mass of exactly 0.5 (a coin flip: both sides prove one half) stays on the result but moves no score (gate 05.09.2026). The solver that supplies the children also serves as a ground truth for small endgames in the bank tooling (the synthetic endgames of `ENDGAME_FIXTURES`, `endgame-truth.spec.ts`); it does not run in the app. In round 35 the prover cut the Brier of the search against the 14 exactly solved positions from 0.0632 to 0.0243, and proved 33 of 816 bank positions, 30 of them right; the three proofs that lost (2663114316 turns 18 and 22, 751443 turn 23) were wrong in the search before. Chance is priced as outcome classes with one representative child per class, as in the matrix cells.

## Considered options

- A clamp that pins a decided score to plus or minus 0.85 (the "Decided-Klammer"): rejected, because the decided side wins only 80 %. The same clamp (floor 0.85) was built and reverted in round 15, re-measured in round 50 under a hold rule (about 2 bp against a bank resolution of 23 bp) and in T55 (at most 3 bp), and the triage discarded it.
- A state budget instead of a cell budget: the first budget cost 2 s per failed position on the bank, up to 20 s with nine answers. The round's lesson: a state budget does not measure cost, cells do.
- A one-seed probe, an early exit while the static stands, a cost-estimate gate and 150 cells: all measured in round 35 and dropped (too optimistic, or they cost proofs, or no change).
- The static estimate alone in the endgame: it read the wrong side on 749828 turn 23, which the prover corrected.
- Hand lists for the defending side: excluded; the defender gets all legal answers.

## Consequences

The prover fits only the class model (hit, miss, kill roll, tie order): crits are left out of the model and some defensive lines stay open, so 573756 turn 138 proved at 0.88 in round 35 and stayed under the speaking threshold (`SPOKEN_MASS`). Doubles fall back to sampled rolls. The score changes in endgames, so the cache version bumped in round 35 (v41).
