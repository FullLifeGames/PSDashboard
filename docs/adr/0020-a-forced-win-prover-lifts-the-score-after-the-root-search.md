# A forced-win prover plays the position out after the root search and lifts the score by the proven mass

Where the board is small (up to three living bodies, or the last pair) or the decided sweep or the near stage names a side and the search already reads it at `PROVER_SCORE_FLOOR` or beyond, the app plays each answer through the simulator to the end of the game after the root search and says "X wins in N against every reply", with the dice share in the sentence ("if the 95 % Fire Fang lands", "barring a crit"). The proof is an AND/OR search over the children that the endgame solver builds (`endgame/`), with a memo on the endgame key and a budget counted in matrix cells (`PROVER_BUDGET`: 100 cells per position, 200 states, depth 12, no wall clock, so results stay deterministic; since round 89 the root tries the ranking's first three moves with the full proof, singles and doubles alike, and no greedy probe gates them). The proven mass (the share of dice outcomes along which the side wins whatever the opponent answers) lifts the score: `score = mass * sign + (1 - mass) * openValue`, where the open branches keep the static value. Matrix, ranking and interval stay as the search left them. A mass of exactly 0.5 (a coin flip: both sides prove one half) stays on the result but moves no score (gate 05.09.2026). The solver that supplies the children also serves as a ground truth for small endgames in the bank tooling (the synthetic endgames of `ENDGAME_FIXTURES`, `endgame-truth.spec.ts`); it does not run in the app. In round 35 the prover cut the Brier of the search against the 14 exactly solved positions from 0.0632 to 0.0243, and proved 33 of 816 bank positions, 30 of them right; the three proofs that lost (2663114316 turns 18 and 22, 751443 turn 23) were wrong in the search before. Chance is priced as outcome classes with one representative child per class, as in the matrix cells.

## Considered options

- A clamp that pins a decided score to plus or minus 0.85 (the "Decided-Klammer"): rejected, because the decided side wins only 80 %. The same clamp (floor 0.85) was built and reverted in round 15, re-measured in round 50 under a hold rule (about 2 bp against a bank resolution of 23 bp) and in T55 (at most 3 bp), and the triage discarded it.
- A state budget instead of a cell budget: the first budget cost 2 s per failed position on the bank, up to 20 s with nine answers. The round's lesson: a state budget does not measure cost, cells do.
- A one-seed probe, an early exit while the static stands, a cost-estimate gate and 150 cells: all measured in round 35 and dropped (too optimistic, or they cost proofs, or no change).
- A greedy five-turn probe (16 cells for the three root candidates) that decided whether the full proof starts: it was part of round 35's design and dropped in round 89 (A5f-6, A5f-7). It missed 8 to 20 % of the doubles proofs the full proof finds (one to three turns long, all for the side that won), because a doubles root has 6 to 16 replies and the shared 16 cells paid for about one turn of one candidate; singles missed none, and the full proof for the three candidates costs 0.3 to 1 % more search forks.
- The static estimate alone in the endgame: it read the wrong side on 749828 turn 23, which the prover corrected.
- Hand lists for the defending side: excluded; the defender gets all legal answers.

## Consequences

The prover fits only the class model (hit, miss, kill roll, tie order): crits are left out of the model and some defensive lines stay open, so 573756 turn 138 proved at 0.88 in round 35 and stayed under the speaking threshold (`SPOKEN_MASS`). Doubles fall back to sampled rolls: the plain path draws the cell's own seeds and books a proof only when every draw agrees. Three agreeing draws booked a Protect that holds one time in three as a sure failure; since round 89 doubles draw five and singles keep three, one table in `endgame/children.ts`. The disclosed limit: five draws that all miss a roll the simulator holds one time in three still happen in 32 of 243 cells (three draws: 8 of 27), and a singles cell with a stalling move keeps the three-draw limit until a measurement names another count. The score changes in endgames, so the cache version bumped in round 35 (v41).


## Amended (round 89)

Round 89 moved four rules of the prover (#59, #145; the lane's step names are A5f and A6).

- **Three root candidates, a lazy pass first.** The root tries the ranking's first three moves, singles and doubles alike, and no greedy probe gates them (see "Considered options"). Each candidate runs a lazy pass first: the children the plain path turned by a die are left aside, so its mass is a lower bound of the full proof's and a candidate under `MIN_FORCED_MASS` stops there. A candidate above it gets the full pass, and the better mass stands (`prover.ts`, `candidateMass`). The three share the 100-cell budget.
- **A die the simulator gives no price for stays unpriced.** A plain group whose draws differ in the faints or in the game's end, or whose turn drew one die more than once (a contact ability, a repeated Protect), is unpriced, and no proof runs through it (`endgame/children.ts`, `plainGroup`). Where the draws agree and the shares are known, the child carries the simulator's own chance, so a repeated Protect splits a cell by the chance it passes to `randomChance`.
- **A proof below mass 1 stays visible** at or above `MIN_FORCED_MASS`, for a side that lost its game too, and the report, which still speaks from `SPOKEN_MASS`, names the share below mass 1 as the card does (`forcedQualifier` in `prose/phrases.ts`: "in 98% of the rolls").
- **Draw counts.** The plain path draws five seeds per damaging doubles pair and three in singles (`PLAIN_DRAWS`). The disclosed limit stays: a damage roll or a crit that does not decide a knockout is not priced, so a doubles proof can rest on it (user ruling of 10.11.2026).

The two rules together cost about 2.5 times the prover's time on the 163 positions of the lane's sample, which adds about 1.3 % to a doubles search. The card does not name a stall: no new `ForcedWinOpen` kind and no change to `summary.ts` (user ruling of 11.11.2026).
